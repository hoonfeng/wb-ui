fix: flex auto-height center alignment + margin-left:auto overflow

Fixes the token panel cache-ring position and right-panel text clipping:

1. align-items:center/flex-end on a flex ROW with auto height used the
   container's still-unmeasured content height (ch) as the centering
   reference. When a child was TALLER than that (96px cache-ring in a
   body measured at ~16px), (ch-bh)/2 went negative and lifted the ring
   ABOVE the panel title (ring top 377.7 < header top 383.9, overlapping
   the "Token 统计" header). Clamp the reference to the child size so
   oversized children align to the content top instead of overflowing.
   Ring now sits at body top + padding (417), body grows to 123.4px.

2. margin-left:auto in flex rows computed its free space as
   `cw - totalMain`, which double-counted the inter-item gaps already
   accumulated in mainPos, pushing auto-margin items past the content
   edge: conv-stats-total right=1284 > conv-sidebar right=1280, clipping
   its text (the "no window margin / text truncated" report). Now the
   free space is `cw - (mainPos-cx) - finalMainSize`, so auto-margin
   items end flush at the content edge (right=1272). Frame content width
   drops to 1280 — no horizontal scrollbar.
